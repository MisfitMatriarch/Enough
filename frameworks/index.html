Acknowledgement of Country

Sovereignty was never ceded. No treaty was ever signed. Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples are the Traditional Custodians of the lands and waters on which this work is made, and that does not become less true with time, legislation or silence.

FREE 2B ME acknowledges Elders past, present and emerging. This body of work exists because systems cause harm when they ask people to become easier to accommodate. No community in this country has been asked to do that more often, or for longer, than First Nations peoples.

We hold that as a matter of practice, not sentiment.

Frameworks & Tools

What does my practice require the person to become?

These are the tools for answering that honestly. Each one does a specific job inside the Practice. None of them is the Practice.

The FREE 2B ME Practice is broader than any one framework or tool. These resources help practitioners and people examine particular parts of practice - such as access, interpretation, body and sensory considerations, conditions, reflection and decision-making. They are not interchangeable, and none of them represents the whole FREE 2B ME Practice.

Each resource has its own purpose, evidence basis, development status, intended users and scope boundaries.

These resources do not automatically constitute clinical, diagnostic, functional-capacity or NDIS assessments. Each resource identifies its intended use, evidence basis, development status and limitations. Author-developed tools are identified as such.

Access comes before interpretation.

Access can materially affect what a person is able to communicate, demonstrate, understand, participate in or use in a particular situation. That is why FREE 2B ME treats access as something to examine before making strong conclusions about unwillingness, skill, capacity, behaviour or intervention need. Access is not the absence of challenge.

Access Before Intervention

Before interpreting difficulty as resistance, incapacity, unwillingness or behaviour requiring change, consider whether the person can meaningfully access what is being asked - communication, information, relationship, sensory or physical access, timing, pace, modality and support.

Access Before Intervention does not mean intervention never occurs. Once access is sufficiently addressed, teaching, treatment, skill development, challenge or other intervention may still be appropriate where it serves the person’s goals and is justified within scope.

Four Access Levers

REDUCE · PROTECT · RESTORE · ADD. A practical way of considering what might change around access in a particular situation. The levers are one tool for thinking about access, not the whole implementation model of FREE 2B ME.

Support that doesn’t become the author.

The person’s knowledge, preferences, goals and authority remain foundational throughout the Practice. Professional expertise, evidence, support and systems can add something useful without becoming the author of the person’s life. Sovereignty is an important principle within FREE 2B ME, not the organising structure of every framework and tool.

A framework is not automatically a service

FREE 2B ME frameworks may inform thinking across different contexts, but using an idea or tool does not turn an interaction into counselling, Specialist Behaviour Support, therapy or another professional service. Professional services remain subject to individual assessment, practitioner qualifications, professional scope, consent, evidence requirements and relevant regulation.

How practitioners learn to use the tools

FREE 2B ME Practitioners do not become competent simply by reading a framework. The Practitioner Pathway connects learning with supervised application, self-application, observation, feedback and capability evidence.

Learning complete ≠ capability demonstrated ≠ participant-specific readiness ≠ Work Authority.

Nervous System Nourishment

What it is, and is not

Nervous System Nourishment is an author-developed body-and-access framework for exploring sensory, movement, rest, communication, relational, environmental and practical factors that may influence access, comfort, participation and wellbeing. It does not assume that one nervous-system state explains a person’s experience, and it does not make calm the goal. It may sit alongside counselling, Specialist Behaviour Support, medical care or allied-health work where relevant. It does not replace them.

It is currently being critically refined through supervised postgraduate work and wider professional review. It is not: a calming protocol; a treatment for Autism or ADHD; compulsory interoception or body awareness; forced mindfulness or breathwork; a trauma-release treatment; a substitute for counselling, Specialist Behaviour Support, medical care or allied-health care; or a validated standalone treatment. Its components draw on existing areas of practice and evidence, but the combined framework should not be mistaken for an independently validated clinical intervention.

Evidence & review

Built to be questioned

FREE 2B ME does not treat author-developed language as evidence simply because it has a name. Tools and frameworks are reviewed against research, professional standards, lived experience, implementation evidence and what happens in real practice. Development status and limitations should remain visible.
