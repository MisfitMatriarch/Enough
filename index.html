Acknowledgement of Country

Sovereignty was never ceded. No treaty was ever signed. Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples are the Traditional Custodians of the lands and waters on which this work is made, and that does not become less true with time, legislation or silence.

FREE 2B ME acknowledges Elders past, present and emerging. This body of work exists because systems cause harm when they ask people to become easier to accommodate. No community in this country has been asked to do that more often, or for longer, than First Nations peoples.

We hold that as a matter of practice, not sentiment.

Less fixing.
More living.

Not everything that is hard about your life belongs to you to fix.

FREE 2B ME brings together books, practice, tools, learning and systems change so people can live, belong and grow without being treated as problems to fix.

Maybe you were never the whole problem.

You can spend years learning to cope better, communicate better, work harder, recover faster and need less.

Sometimes those things help.

And sometimes the question nobody asked was whether the life around you needed to change too.

The FREE 2B ME Practice

What if the person is not the automatic problem?

When life becomes difficult, the answer is not always to ask the person to try harder, cope better or become easier to support.

The FREE 2B ME Practice considers the whole situation: what the person needs, what is already working, what is getting in the way and what could change around them.

Explore the Practice

More than one place

Change can happen in more than one place.

Across the person, relationships, support, environments, professional practice and systems.

Sometimes the person needs a skill. Sometimes the space, the pace, a relationship or the system needs to change. Often it is more than one of them at once.

Ideas that keep coming back

The cost of looking fine.

Visible functioning does not tell us what it cost, what made it possible or what happens afterwards.

Capacity is conditional.

What was possible yesterday is information. It is not a contract for today.

Whose change is this?

Sometimes change belongs with the person. Sometimes with support, relationships, environments or systems. Often it is shared.

  • Preparation.

Founder · Author · Speaker · Practitioner

Tanya Hicks

Human Systems Architect.

I know what it costs to spend years trying to become easier for the world to hold.

As a late-identified AuDHD woman living with disability, chronic illness and trauma, and as a single mother of a transgender daughter, I have seen how difference becomes something to correct, and how much life is lost when belonging depends on becoming smaller, quieter or easier to understand.

I stopped trying to fix myself into acceptability and began building what I could not find: places, practices and pathways where people can belong without becoming less of themselves.

That became FREE 2B ME Global: Less fixing. More living.

It lives in The Nest, where therapeutic practice meets shared interests, relationships and community life; in the Practitioner Pathway, developing people to carry neuroaffirming practice with capability, humility and accountability into different professions and systems; and in books, learning and tools changing how people are understood and supported.

Why I build

My story is about turning lived experience into infrastructure so our daughters inherit more than encouragement. They inherit different possibilities.

The aim is not to make people resilient enough to tolerate damaging systems. It is to change what the system asks people to endure.
