The idea

What if the person isn’t the only thing that could change?

People are often asked to try harder, cope better, calm down, communicate differently, be more independent, tolerate more or fit in better.

Sometimes learning, treatment or personal change genuinely helps. And sometimes the thing getting in the way is somewhere else.

FREE 2B ME asks what is happening, what matters to the person, what is making this harder, what could change, and who is actually responsible for changing it.

Change what needs changing. Don’t make the person the default target.

One ecosystem

One ecosystem. Different ways it comes to life.

FREE 2B ME is not one service, one framework or one programme. It connects the different parts of support and practice that too often operate separately — the person’s own knowledge and goals, everyday support, professional services, learning, evidence, workforce capability and the systems that hold practice accountable.

The core practice statement

Begin with the person. Add what is useful.

Begin with the person’s knowledge of their own life. Add useful support, skills, evidence and professional expertise without replacing their authority.

A person may want to learn something, build a skill, strengthen a relationship, communicate differently, solve a problem, access treatment, use practical support, change something around them or challenge a system that is getting in the way.

Good practice does not decide the answer in advance. It asks: what needs to change, develop, be supported, be understood or be redesigned — and who has the responsibility and authority to act?

What can actually change?

There is more than one place to look.

Something I want to learn or practise

Skills, communication, confidence, everyday capability, self-advocacy or something else that matters.

My body or health

Pain, sleep, fatigue, medication, movement, sensory needs, physical health or the state I want to access.

Communication

How information is given, how I express myself, processing time, AAC, written communication or another route.

Relationships and support

How people listen, respond, help, share responsibility and repair things when they go wrong.

The place around me

Noise, lighting, layout, timing, predictability, transport, crowding, demands or physical access.

Professional support

Counselling, Specialist Behaviour Support, medical care or another professional service where it is actually needed.

Services and systems

Rules, staffing, processes, expectations, policies or organisational decisions one person cannot change alone.

Sometimes the work sits with the person. Sometimes it sits around them. Often it sits in more than one place.

Capability

Capability without conformity

Less fixing does not mean no learning, no challenge, no development and no intervention.

People may want to build communication, planning, practical skills, self-advocacy, decision-making, relationships, confidence, ways of navigating change, support-seeking or other capability that matters in their life.

At the same time, people should not be required to learn skills merely to appear more typical, compliant, independent or convenient.

We build capability without making conformity the goal.

The FREE 2B ME Practice

Understand before you intervene.

The Practice uses a repeatable reasoning process: Notice → Separate → Locate → Make Accessible → Choose Direction → Support & Implement → Review → Recalibrate.

This is not a programme a person has to move through. It is a practice discipline for keeping support individual, accessible, purposeful and accountable.

The work might sit with the person, a skill, communication, health, a relationship, support, professional practice, the environment, an organisation or a wider system.

Continuity

There is no requirement to graduate from belonging.

Autism is lifelong. Support needs can change across a person’s life. Someone may need more support at one time, less at another, or a different kind of support as life changes.

A counselling episode may end. A Behaviour Support intervention may change. A particular funded support may start or stop. But useful community, communication access, sensory-responsive environments and belonging do not become failures simply because they continue.

Success is not measured by how completely support disappears.

Continuity is available, not compulsory. People can leave, return, pause, change how they participate or choose somewhere else. Support can change without belonging having to end.

Professional depth

Different questions need different kinds of expertise.

FREE 2B ME is not anti-professional. It is anti-professional takeover.

Depending on what is happening, useful work may involve counselling, Specialist Behaviour Support, psychology, psychiatry, medicine, pharmacy, occupational therapy, speech pathology, physiotherapy, social work, disability support or community support.

The questions are: who knows what? Who is responsible for what? What does the person know about their own life? Who owns the next decision?

About me

I am the creator and architect of FREE 2B ME - an Autistic author and practitioner, Human Systems Architect, counsellor and NDIS Behaviour Support Practitioner, framework developer, researcher and speaker.

I founded Neurodivergent Empowered and Sparkly Aliens Inc., and I am the author of the FREE 2B ME Practice Library. My work grew from lived experience, professional practice and years of seeing neurodivergent people expected to adapt to systems that had never been designed with them in mind. Rather than build another isolated programme, I began connecting the pieces - real-life support, counselling, Specialist Behaviour Support, evidence, books, practitioner learning, workforce systems and governance.

Counsellor (ACA R82053), NDIS Behaviour Support Practitioner (P1362818), and Master of Counselling candidate. I created the Sovereignty Conditions Audit.
