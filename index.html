The idea

What if the person isn’t the only thing that could change?

People are often asked to try harder, cope better, calm down, communicate differently, be more independent, tolerate more or fit in better.

Sometimes learning, treatment or personal change genuinely helps. And sometimes the thing getting in the way is somewhere else.

FREE 2B ME asks what is happening, what matters to the person, what is making this harder, what could change, and who is actually responsible for changing it.

Change what needs changing. Don’t make the person the default target.

What can actually change?

There is more than one place to look.

Something I want to learn or practise

Skills, communication, confidence, everyday capability, self-advocacy or something else that matters.

My body or health

Pain, sleep, fatigue, medication, movement, sensory needs, physical health or the state I want to access.

Communication

How information is given, how I express myself, processing time, AAC, written communication or another route.

Relationships and support

How people listen, respond, help, share responsibility and repair things when they go wrong.

The place around me

Noise, lighting, layout, timing, predictability, transport, crowding, demands or physical access.

Professional support

Counselling, Specialist Behaviour Support, medical care or another professional service where it is actually needed.

Services and systems

Rules, staffing, processes, expectations, policies or organisational decisions one person cannot change alone.

Sometimes the work sits with the person. Sometimes it sits around them. Often it sits in more than one place.

The person is not the only one expected to change.

Support should be able to change too. So should the practitioner, the environment, the service, the workforce, the documentation, the technology and the organisation.

Capability without conformity

Learn what you want to learn.
Not what makes you easier to be around.

People may want to learn, build skills, accept support, use treatment or make changes that matter to them.

That is different from requiring someone to appear more typical, compliant, independent or convenient.

Continuity

There is no requirement to graduate from belonging.

People do not have to outgrow support or become someone else to belong. What support looks like can change across a person’s life. Someone may need more support at one time, less at another, or a different kind as life changes.

A counselling episode may end. A Behaviour Support intervention may change. A particular funded support may start or stop. Ongoing community, communication access, sensory-responsive environments and useful support are not signs that someone has failed to progress.

Success is not measured by how completely support disappears.

Continuity is available, not compulsory. People can leave, return, pause, change how they participate or choose somewhere else. Support can change without belonging having to end.

About

About me

I am the creator and architect of FREE 2B ME - an Autistic author and practitioner, Human Systems Architect, counsellor and NDIS Behaviour Support Practitioner, framework developer, researcher and speaker.

I founded Neurodivergent Empowered and Sparkly Aliens Inc., and I am the author of the FREE 2B ME Practice Library. My work grew from lived experience, professional practice and years of seeing neurodivergent people expected to adapt to systems that had never been designed with them in mind. Rather than build another isolated programme, I began connecting the pieces - real-life support, counselling, Specialist Behaviour Support, evidence, books, practitioner learning, workforce systems and governance.

Counsellor (ACA R82053), NDIS Behaviour Support Practitioner (P1362818), and Master of Counselling candidate. I created the Sovereignty Conditions Audit.
