Speaking

Keynotes, presentations & conversations that change how people think about support

Tanya Hicks speaks about neuroaffirming practice, disability, behaviour, capability, access, identity, professional responsibility and the systems that shape people’s everyday lives. What Tanya brings into the room

Tanya’s speaking combines lived experience with professional practice, humour, uncomfortable questions and concrete examples of what support looks like when theory meets real life. She does not present neurodivergent people as case studies to be fixed. She asks audiences to examine their own practice, assumptions, authority and systems - while still leaving them with something practical to do next.

Lived experience · professional practice · systems thinking · practical application · accessible delivery

Accessible by design

Tanya’s presentations can be adapted for different audiences, processing styles and event formats. Options may include accessible slides, advance materials, transcript or written summary, structured Q&A, reduced sensory load, and workshop formats that do not depend on rapid verbal participation.

Talks can be delivered as keynotes, conference presentations, workshops or keynote-plus-workshop packages depending on audience and purpose. Confirmed event history is provided on enquiry.
