FREE 2B ME professional learning is designed to help you feel more confident in your decisions, have more useful conversations and work with autistic and other neurodivergent clients on support that fits their lives.

Less second-guessing. More confidence in where to start.

The person behind FREE 2B ME

Professional practice and lived experience, brought together.

FREE 2B ME was created by Tanya Hicks, a late-identified autistic and ADHD woman, counsellor and founder of The Nest. Tanya brings her experience as a practitioner and a neurodivergent person to the question at the heart of this work: how can support make more room for the person?

Her work extends across counselling, behaviour support practice, practitioner development, speaking and the FREE 2B ME books. It is grounded in the belief that people deserve support that fits their lives, not pressure to become someone else.

WHEN YOUR USUAL APPROACH ISN’T WORKING

Access Before Intervention

Foundations of Neuroaffirming Practice

Who it’s for: counsellors, allied health professionals and educators working with autistic and other neurodivergent people.

Your client misses appointments, goes quiet or struggles with a plan that made sense in the session. You want to help, but you’re running out of ideas.

Build confidence in what to do when a session or plan isn’t working for an autistic or neurodivergent client.

What this can mean for your practice:

  • Feel more confident when a client goes quiet, misses sessions or can’t use a plan
  • Work out what to change in the session instead of repeating what isn’t working
  • Build plans with clients that better fit their day-to-day lives

WHEN YOUR CLIENT SAYS THEY’RE FINE

Behind the Mask

Identity, Safety and Authenticity in Practice

Who it’s for: counsellors, psychologists, social workers and allied health professionals working with autistic and other neurodivergent people.

Your client looks like they’re coping. Then you discover they have been exhausted or struggling for weeks. How do you make room for the conversation you may be missing?

Build confidence in understanding what a client needs, even when the full picture isn’t obvious in the room.

What this can mean for your practice:

  • Feel more confident starting conversations when a client seems fine but may be struggling
  • Understand more of what matters to your client without pressuring them to disclose
  • Make plans together that reflect the effort and support behind their day

A$2,700 per participant

WHEN EVERY SUGGESTION IS MET WITH A NO

From Compliance to Collaboration

Autonomy and Demand in Practice

Who it’s for: counsellors, allied health professionals, educators and team leaders supporting neurodivergent children, young people and adults.

You offer ideas, set goals and try to move forward, but the person withdraws or gets upset. You don’t want to push harder, and you need another way to help.

Feel more confident finding a way forward when a client says no and your usual suggestions aren’t helping.

What this can mean for your practice:

  • Know where to start when a client says no or withdraws from a plan
  • Keep working together without relying on pressure or compliance
  • Navigate disagreement and professional responsibilities with more confidence

Beyond Burnout

Body, Capacity and Sustainable Support

Who it’s for: counsellors and allied health professionals supporting autistic and other neurodivergent people experiencing exhaustion or burnout.

Your client can no longer manage things they used to do. The strategies that once helped now feel like another demand, and you’re unsure where to start.

Know where to start when an autistic or neurodivergent client is exhausted and the usual plans aren’t helping.
