Acknowledgement of Country

Sovereignty was never ceded. No treaty was ever signed. Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples are the Traditional Custodians of the lands and waters on which this work is made, and that does not become less true with time, legislation or silence.

FREE 2B ME acknowledges Elders past, present and emerging. This body of work exists because systems cause harm when they ask people to become easier to accommodate. No community in this country has been asked to do that more often, or for longer, than First Nations peoples.

We hold that as a matter of practice, not sentiment.

FREE 2B ME · Professional learning

Your client says they’re fine. You’re not sure they are.

They turn up. They answer your questions. They agree to the plan. On paper, the session went well. But something stays with you after they leave. What might you be missing, and what do you do next?

For the moments that stay with you

Because caring deeply does not always tell you what to do next.

You can know the language, bring years of experience and want the best for every person you support. You can still leave a session questioning whether your usual approach is helping, or whether it is asking more of the person than you can see.

When the plan stops working

You have prepared carefully, but your client withdraws or cannot use the plan in everyday life. What needs to change first?

When coping has a cost

The person appears fine. You want to understand their experience without assuming what is happening beneath the surface.

When you need more than words

You want greater confidence in what to notice, what to ask and how to adapt your practice with the person.

Those questions do not make you a bad practitioner. They tell you that you are paying attention. What matters is what you do next.

Become the practitioner who knows where to look next.

FREE 2B ME professional learning invites you to go deeper. Explore how to recognise barriers, make thoughtful decisions and create conditions where the person has a genuine voice in the work.

Bring greater curiosity, clarity and confidence to the moments that matter.
