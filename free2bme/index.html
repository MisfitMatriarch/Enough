Your details and your choices

Your name, email and chosen titles are used to manage your reservation and contact you about those books. Optional marketing consent is recorded separately. We do not collect your reflection answers.

Reservation records are held privately in Netlify storage and accessed by authorised staff. Netlify hosts this website and processes technical requests. Our own reservation handler does not log your contact details. Storage may be outside Australia.

To ask about your details, request a correction or deletion, change your reservation, or withdraw marketing consent, email sparkly@neurodivergentempowered.com. Please include the email you used for your reservation. We keep reservation details while needed to manage your selected books and related enquiries.
