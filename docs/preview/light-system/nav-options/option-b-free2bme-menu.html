THE FREE 2B ME PRACTICE

Understand before
you intervene.

FREE 2B ME is a neuroaffirming practice for working out what is happening, what matters to the person, where useful change belongs and who has the responsibility and authority to act.

It begins with the person's own knowledge of their life.
