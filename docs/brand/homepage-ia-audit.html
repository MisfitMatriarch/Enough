The actual finding. A visitor is asked to choose from a card grid three separate times — #find-your-way-in (6 routes), #ecosystem (9 parts) and #homes (8 destinations). Seventeen of those cards name overlapping things. The Nest, Neurodivergent Empowered, the Practitioner Pathway, the Practice and the books each appear twice, in different groupings, with different framing.

That is what makes the page feel like it must be decoded. Removing the word “sovereignty” would not touch it.

Finding · scope contradiction

The homepage tells two different scope stories.

Most of it presents FREE 2B ME as a broad human system with no diagnosis requirement. Three passages then narrow the whole work to neurodivergent people, which reads backwards through everything above them as if the broad language were only shorthand.

I swept every scope-bearing phrase in the page body — neurodivergent, neuroaffirming, autistic, autism, disability, diagnosis, neurotype. Fifteen occurrences. Twelve are legitimate — the proper noun Neurodivergent Empowered, book subtitles, the origin story, a professional-discipline list, and the audit tool's “not diagnostic” disclaimer. Three set an eligibility boundary. They are exactly the three already identified.

#find-your-way-in — “I support someone”
Ideas for families, supporters and people working alongside neurodivergent people.

Gates a primary entry route by neurotype.

Proposed
Ideas for families, supporters and people working alongside someone who needs support.
#continuity
Autism is lifelong. Support needs can change across a person's life.

The argument — that ongoing support is not failure — does not depend on autism. Anchoring it there narrows a universal point.

Proposed
Some things about a person do not go away, and do not need to. Support needs still change across a life.
#homes — the closing promise
Neurodivergent people do not need another system built around correcting them.

The most scope-defining sentence on the page, and the last thing a visitor reads.

Proposed
People do not need another system built around correcting them.

Deleting two words makes the line broader and stronger.

The rest of the site is already correct. /the-practice/ and /for-organisations/ contain zero scope terms. /the-practice/deeper-architecture/ has one — “not a diagnostic assessment”, a disclaimer. /practitioner-pathway/ has one — neurodivergence listed as one capability domain beside trauma, mental health and medication literacy, which is curriculum, not eligibility. The <head> says “neuroaffirming practice ecosystem”: a commitment, not a boundary.

So the contradiction is confined to the homepage and to three sentences. This is a surgical correction, not a rewrite.

Proposed structure

Nine sections. One navigation system.

Layers 1 and 2 on the homepage. Layers 3 and 4 move to /the-practice/ and /the-practice/deeper-architecture/, which exist precisely to hold them.

  1. Less fixing. More living.

    The front door, untouched. Headline, the challenge question, one short paragraph, two actions.

    from #top — unchanged

  2. What if the person isn't the only thing that could change?

    Already the strongest writing on the site. Concrete, no jargon, recognisable while exhausted.

    from #the-idea — unchanged

  3. There is more than one place to look.

    Seven cards in the person's own voice — “My body or health”, “The place around me”. This is the architecture translated. It should stay exactly as written.

    from #what-can-change — unchanged

  4. Find your way in.

    Five role routes, one human sentence each. Australia moves out of the card set into a single supporting line — it is a geography question, not a role, and as a peer card it forces a comparison nobody needs to make yet.

    from #find-your-way-in — 6 cards → 5 + 1 line

  5. The person is not the only one expected to change.

    The dark band. Short, strong, already right.

    from #manifesto — unchanged

  6. There is no requirement to graduate from belonging.

    Answers a question families genuinely carry. Trim by roughly a third.

    from #continuity — shortened

  7. One body of work. Different ways in.

    The single replacement for both card grids: Books · The Practice · Practitioner Learning · Tools · Speaking. One human sentence each, then one quiet link — “See how the whole ecosystem fits together →”. Architecture becomes available, not an entry requirement.

    merges #ecosystem + #homes + #frameworks + #speaking + #core-practice — 20 cards → 5

  8. Ideas you can live with.

    The four person-facing books with one human sentence each. The four professional companions revealed on interaction or linked, not presented as eight titles at once.

    from #library — 312 words → ~150

  9. About Tanya

    Kept, shortened, and “Human Systems Architect” given a plain-English companion line. Credentials stay — a professional reader is looking for them.

    from #about — translated

Result: 17 sections → 9, 33 cards → 17, ~2,085 words → ~1,250. Nothing legitimate is deleted. #outward, #professional-depth and the keynote's conceptual lines move to pages built for them.

Copy direction

Translate for meaning, not word-for-word.

Now — #outward
The Practice uses a repeatable reasoning process: Notice → Separate → Locate → Make Accessible → Choose Direction → Support & Implement → Review → Recalibrate.
Homepage
Work out what is happening before deciding what to do about it.

The eight named steps are real and stay — on /the-practice/, where a practitioner is looking for them. On the homepage they ask a frightened parent to learn a process before they know whether this place is safe.

Now — #frameworks
The collaborative creation and protection of the conditions that support access, while preserving choice, bodily integrity and self-determination.
Homepage
Making things easier to reach, without taking the decisions away from the person.

A definition written for citation, doing frontstage work. The precise wording belongs in the framework page it defines.

Now — #keynote
Capacity follows conditions. Sovereignty follows safety.
Homepage
People can do more when life works with them instead of against them.

Then the original line sits underneath in smaller type as the keynote's own words. Both layers present; the plain one leads.

Now — #really-about
We build capability without making conformity the goal.
Homepage
Learn what you want to learn. Not what makes you easier to be around.

Same argument, sharper, and repeatable — someone could say it out loud about their own life.

What I will not do. Flatten it. “Capability without conformity” is a good phrase and the #what-can-change cards are already exemplary. The risk in this pass is replacing precise writing with generic wellbeing copy, and I would rather leave a strong sentence containing a framework word than trade it for something emptier.

Before I write anything

Four decisions.

  • Does the merged “One body of work” section replace both card grids? This is the single biggest change and the one that fixes the decode problem. It means #homes disappears as a section and its eight destinations are represented by five routes plus a link to the full ecosystem.
  • Sparkly Aliens Inc. and Evidence & Review. Both currently appear only in the card grids being merged. Do they need homepage presence, or is the ecosystem page the right home?
  • Books — four or eight? I propose four person-facing titles visible, four professional companions one interaction away. Tell me if all eight must stay visible.
  • Scope. Homepage only this pass, or do the interior pages follow? They carry the same vocabulary. I would do the homepage first and measure.

On /the-practice/deeper-architecture/: I am treating it as exempt. It exists to hold the architecture, and thinning it would defeat the layering.
