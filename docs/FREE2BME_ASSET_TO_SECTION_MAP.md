# FREE 2B ME homepage — asset to section map

One source per major section. Every placement is a crop, band, edge or contained panel. No complete
chapter plate is shown, no chapter name or number appears, nothing is used full-bleed, and nothing is
reused across unrelated sections. Rendered proof of all fifteen crops:
`FREE2BME_crop_map_proof_2026-09-11.jpg`.

## The upscaling rule this map is built to

Every plate is 1024 x 1536. A crop therefore has a hard ceiling: **its maximum rendered width is half
its pixel width**, so a two-times display still reads native. That ceiling is why each section below
is a band, an edge or a contained panel rather than a backdrop, and it is what keeps the layout
cream-led. Column widths are set from the artwork, not the artwork stretched to the column.

## Three sub-slots from one plate, and why

Learning's three routes are three overlapping crops of the **same** spiral. That is deliberate and is
the only place one plate serves more than one slot. The routes have to read as one environment with
three ways through it, and three unrelated pieces of chapter art would say the opposite. They are
sub-slots of a single section, not separate sections.

## Assets that stay out of the public pages

- **The eleven titled plates** (`ch2` to `ch13`, 1024 x 1536) — chapter names and numbers printed in
  the artwork. Source material only.
- **`fb-cover.png`** — carries the logo and `WWW.FREE2BME.GLOBAL` baked into the image. The real
  transparent logo is placed as its own element instead.
- **`brand-cover-art.png`** — not required by any section.
- **`assets/awards/` (39 badge files)** — excluded by the recognition ruling.
- **The 520 x 500 logo from the code handoff** — cream baked in. Superseded by `Logo TM.png`.
