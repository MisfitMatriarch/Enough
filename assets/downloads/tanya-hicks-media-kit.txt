LONG BIO
Tanya Hicks is the founder of FREE 2B ME, an MC, speaker, author and practitioner. Her work connects identity, belonging, access and the possibilities people want for their own lives. She hosts conversations, speaks at events, writes books and develops learning and practice with room for different ways of participating. As a late-identified AuDHD woman living with disability and chronic illness, she brings lived experience alongside her practice work. Her interests include women’s empowerment, LGBTQI+ belonging and helping other people share what they know. The Nest in Caloundra is operated by Neurodivergent Empowered. She has completed an eight-book FREE 2B ME library, with publication forthcoming. Readers can reserve a copy for A$39, with no payment until launch.
