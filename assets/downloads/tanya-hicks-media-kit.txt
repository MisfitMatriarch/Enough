LONG BIO
Tanya Hicks is a speaker, MC, author, educator and founder of FREE 2B ME. Drawing on neuroscience, yoga, mindfulness, clinical hypnotherapy, counselling and lived experience, she speaks about making the most of the cards we’re dealt and how accommodating our challenges can give us more access to our genius and joy.

Her work spans writing and publishing, teaching, speaking and event hosting, neuroaffirming practice, entrepreneurship and community leadership. She founded Neurodivergent Empowered and serves as President of Sparkly Aliens Inc.

She is a Specialist Positive Behaviour Support Practitioner and a counsellor registered with the Australian Counselling Association at Level 2 (R82053), and a Master of Counselling candidate researching how neurobiology, counselling and positive behaviour support meet in practice. As a late-identified AuDHD woman living with disability and chronic illness, she brings lived experience alongside her practice work. She previously played hockey for Australia Country. She loves MCing because it lets other people have their moment. Her interests include women’s empowerment, LGBTQI+ belonging and helping other people share what they know.

The Nest in Caloundra is operated by Neurodivergent Empowered. She has completed an eight-book FREE 2B ME library, with publication forthcoming. Readers can reserve a copy for A$39, with no payment until launch.
