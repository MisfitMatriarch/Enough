FREE 2B ME Practice Library

Four books for people.
Four books for practice.

The FREE 2B ME Practice Library is the publishing and learning arm of the wider FREE 2B ME ecosystem. Four person-facing books explore what support, difference, distress, capability, relationships and everyday life can look like from the person’s side. Four professional companions ask what practitioners, supervisors and organisations must actually do - including evidence, reasoning, scope, intervention, accountability and implementation. Same territory. Different responsibilities.

The paired library

The same territory. Two different positions.

The person-facing book begins with the experience, knowledge, goals and life of the person. Its professional companion addresses the responsibilities of practitioners and systems: evidence, reasoning, scope, intervention, implementation and accountability. Neither side replaces the other.

Where the books sit

The books are one part of the Practice

The FREE 2B ME Practice Library does not sit separately from the rest of the ecosystem. The same ideas are being applied and tested through the FREE 2B ME Practice, The Nest, Neurodivergent Empowered, the FREE 2B ME Practitioner Pathway, Evidence & Review, and Professional Learning. The books make the thinking visible. The rest of the ecosystem asks whether that thinking can actually hold in practice.

The Practice Library is being developed as a connected publication series. Individual titles will be released as editorial, specialist-review and publication gates are completed. Book one’s current revised edition is FREE 2B ME: Less fixing. More living.
