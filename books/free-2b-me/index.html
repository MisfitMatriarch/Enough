Book 1 · For people

FREE 2B ME

Less fixing. More living.

FREE 2B ME is the connected neuroaffirming practice ecosystem. This is its foundational person-facing book, and it carries the ecosystem’s promise as its subtitle. The repetition is deliberate.

The promise

What this book is for

It begins with the knowledge, strengths, goals, preferences and expertise people already bring to their own lives. It explores how skills, relationships, support, access, opportunity and changes around the person can work together to bring more of the life that matters within reach.

The book does not decide in advance that the person must change or that the environment is always the problem. It asks what needs to be learned, supported, understood, changed or redesigned—and where responsibility and authority sit.

Intended reader

Written for people and families. Professional expertise is brought alongside the reader’s authority over their own life, never in place of it.

Territory

Access, hidden cost and conditions.

The same territory, from the other side

The foundational pair: the person’s authority and life on one side; professional responsibility, access and intervention legitimacy on the other.

What it is not

It does not tell readers who they really are, explain readers to themselves, or offer the only correct map. It does not replace counselling, Specialist Behaviour Support or individual professional advice.

Where it sits
