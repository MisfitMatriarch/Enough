The promise

What this book is for

Done with being told to breathe deeper, meditate harder and just calm down? This book swaps self-management for something better: finding what actually feeds you. The music, the movement, the people, the quiet, the ridiculous joys. Calm is welcome. It’s not the price of entry. This is a book about pleasure, aliveness and a body that gets to enjoy its life.

Intended reader

For anyone exploring what supports their brain, body and the life they want to live.
