The person behind the work

I stopped making acceptability the goal.

I love MCing because I get to help other people have their moment.

As a late-identified AuDHD woman living with disability, chronic illness and trauma, and as a single mother of a transgender daughter, I know what it costs when difference becomes a problem to manage.

I spent years becoming easier for the world to hold. Eventually I stopped organising my life around becoming more acceptable.

Mindfulness and yoga taught me acceptance. Acceptance for all of me, not just the parts that are easy to show.

The cards we’re dealt

You get to decide
what winning means.

I take my cue from Kenny Rogers’s The Gambler. Know what to hold. Know what to fold. Know when to walk away. And know when to go all in on yourself.

I draw on neuroscience, yoga, mindfulness, clinical hypnotherapy, counselling and lived experience. When we accommodate our challenges, we have more access to our genius and joy.

What grew from that

I started building
what I could not find.

Places, practices and pathways where people could belong without becoming less of themselves. FREE 2B ME connects the ideas in my books, talks and teaching with the work I’ve helped build.

Acknowledgement of Country

Sovereignty was never ceded. Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples are the Traditional Custodians of the lands and waters on which this work is made, and that does not become less true with time, legislation or silence.

FREE 2B ME acknowledges Elders past, present and emerging. This body of work exists because systems cause harm when they ask people to become easier to accommodate. No community in this country has been asked to do that more often, or for longer, than First Nations peoples.

We hold that as a matter of practice, not sentiment.
