About Tanya

Meet Tanya Hicks

Award-winning therapist, speaker, author & founder.

Life deals the cards. I teach you how to 🎼 play YOURS right 🎼.

Less fixing. More living.

I take my cue from Kenny Rogers’s The Gambler. Know what to hold. Know what to fold. Know when to walk away. And know when to go all in on yourself.

I draw on neuroscience, yoga, mindfulness, clinical hypnotherapy, counselling and lived experience. When we accommodate our challenges, we have more access to our genius and joy.

That means recognising your strengths, understanding your challenges, making intentional choices and adapting what you do. You get to decide what winning means to you.

I spent years becoming easier for the world to hold.

  • More capable.
  • More accommodating.
  • More explainable.
  • More useful.
  • Less inconvenient.

Eventually I stopped organising my life around becoming more acceptable.

I started building what I could not find: places, practices and pathways where people could belong without becoming less of themselves.

As a late-identified AuDHD woman living with disability, chronic illness and trauma, and as a single mother of a transgender daughter, I know something about what happens when difference becomes a problem to manage.

I also know how much life can disappear when belonging depends on being easier to understand, easier to support, easier to employ, easier to parent, easier to treat, easier to accommodate.

That isn’t the whole story. I’m a mum. I played hockey for Australia Country. I write, dance and sing because I love it, and I love MCing because I get to help other people have their moment. I can speak on an international stage and still need help finding my way there.

Mindfulness and yoga taught me acceptance. Acceptance for all of me, not just the parts that are easy to show.

Being yourself isn’t only a personal decision. Relationships, workplaces, services, money and discrimination can all decide how much room a person has. So I build the conditions, not just the encouragement.

That became FREE 2B ME.

Less fixing. More living.

It is one promise expressed in different places.

    Not just the language. The practice.

Why this matters to me

I did not build this because I found a perfect model.

I built it because I became less interested in asking:

How do I become easier to manage?

and more interested in asking:

What would make more of this life possible without requiring me to become less of myself?

That question changed the direction of my work.

  • What I noticed.
  • What I built.
  • What I stopped accepting as inevitable.
  • What I wanted for my daughter.
  • And what I began to believe other people should be able to expect too.
My story is about turning lived experience into infrastructure so our daughters inherit more than encouragement. They inherit different possibilities.

Not just: be yourself.

  • Places that leave room for it.
  • Practitioners who know how to support it.
  • Organisations that take responsibility for the conditions they create.
  • Communities where difference is not the price of entry.
  • Workplaces where contribution does not depend on disappearing inside the effort.
  • Books and tools that give people language before somebody else defines the experience for them.

That is the work.

Where I stand

Women’s inclusion includes trans women. Queer, non-binary, gender-diverse, intersex and questioning people belong across the wider FREE 2B ME work.

Belonging is not meaningful if some women have to negotiate their right to be there.

Experience & contribution

Professional background and expertise

My work across therapy, teaching, writing, speaking and community leadership comes back to the same question. What can we change about how we respond, or about the conditions around us, so there’s more room for our strengths and joy? Lived experience and neuroaffirming practice shape how I answer it.

  • MC and speakerHosting, keynotes, panels and workshops, with a love of helping other people have their moment.
  • Counselling and behaviour support practiceSpecialist Positive Behaviour Support Practitioner. Counsellor registered with the Australian Counselling Association at Level 2. Master of Counselling candidate. Ongoing supervision and professional accountability.
  • Yoga, mindfulness and clinical hypnotherapyI teach yoga and draw on mindfulness and clinical hypnotherapy to explore attention, acceptance and how we respond to challenges.
  • Neuroscience-informed educationI use ideas from neuroscience to help people notice how attention, stress and context affect what they can do, and what they can change in everyday life.
  • Writing and publishingEight books. Two perspectives. Four begin with the person, four take a professional perspective, and each stands alone. Publication is forthcoming.
  • Practitioner developmentThe Practitioner Pathway, built around capability that can be observed, not attendance alone.
  • Organisational and systems workAccess, conditions, reasoning, implementation and what organisations make possible in ordinary practice.
  • Entrepreneur and community leaderI founded FREE 2B ME and Neurodivergent Empowered, which operates The Nest in Caloundra. I also serve as President of Sparkly Aliens Inc.

You were never the only thing that needed to change.

That belief still shapes the questions I ask now:

  1. What does this leave room for?
  2. Can the person remain themselves here?
  3. What becomes possible when belonging is not something they have to earn by becoming easier?

Those questions run through everything I build.
