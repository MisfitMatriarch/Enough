A free reflection

One cost. One change. More living.

Sometimes people see what you did and miss what it took. This reflection helps you make a fuller account, and notice what you would like more room for.

  • There is no score. It does not diagnose you or decide what you can do.
  • You can skip any question, keep an answer private, or choose Not safe to answer. You don’t have to say why.
  • No email or account. Your words stay on this page and are not sent or saved anywhere.
  • You don’t have to change anything.

What takes more out of me than people realise?

Think of one ordinary thing. What did it take before, during and after?

Some places hidden effort can sit
  • Getting ready, travelling or arriving
  • Light, sound, people, or managing how you appear
  • Finding words, planning, remembering or deciding
  • Pain, fatigue or health
  • Recovering afterwards, or catching up on what got pushed aside
