# Register 03: Books and conversion

Scope: `books/index.html`, the eight `books/*/index.html` pages, `books/pre-order/` and its thank-you page, `be-first-to-know/` and its thank-you page, `stay-connected/` and its thank-you page, `free2bme/index.html` (changes are proposed in `content/expo.json` and `scripts/build-expo.mjs`, not in the generated file), and the homepage `#books` section of `index.html`. No site files were edited.

## Label scheme (applies to every row below)

Today five labels are in use for the two sides: "For living it." / "For practising it." (homepage, FREE 2B ME page, `frameworks/`), "For the person" / "For professional practice" (books index, companion cards), "For people and families" / "For practice" (book page eyebrows), and "Books for professional practice" / "Companion to …" (expo page). Proposed: one pair of labels everywhere.

- **For your life** goes on FREE 2B ME, You Make Sense, Nervous System Nourishment and Built With You.
- **For your practice** goes on Access Before Intervention, Behaviour Makes Sense, Beyond Regulation and Practice That Holds.
- Each pair gets **one connection sentence**, used word for word on both book pages and on the expo card. The sentences come from Tanya’s positioning table:
  - P1 FREE 2B ME / Access Before Intervention: “Read together, they connect the life someone wants with the conditions that enable participation.”
  - P2 You Make Sense / Behaviour Makes Sense: “Read together, they connect experience, context and meaning.”
  - P3 Nervous System Nourishment / Beyond Regulation: “Read together, they connect what sustains someone with what makes participation worthwhile.”
  - P4 Built With You / Practice That Holds: “Read together, they connect authorship, collaborative support and accountable practice.”
- Where the labels go on each page:
  - Book page hero eyebrow.
  - The companion card’s `pair-kicker`, plus the connection sentence directly under the companion section’s `sec-head`.
  - Books index card `<span>`.
  - Homepage series `<span>`.
  - Pre-order quantity labels.
  - Expo route eyebrow, `<summary>` and the companion line on each card.

## Checks

- **Reservation links:** every per-book link already uses `/books/pre-order/?book=<id>#reserve`: 8 on `books/index.html`, 9 on `index.html` (FREE 2B ME appears twice) and 1 on each book page. Every id matches a quantity input `name`, and `assets/js/book-preorder.js` uses that `name` to preselect 1. The generic `/books/pre-order/` links (books index intro, `be-first-to-know`, expo fallback) are deliberately general. No proposal below changes an `href`, an input `name`, or a `?book=` value.
- **Consent wording:** no proposal touches it. That covers the pre-order `contact-consent` label, the `be-first-to-know` consent label, the `stay-connected` visible consent label and its matching hidden `consent-wording` field (they match each other today), the expo `marketingConsentWording`, the `reservationTermsVersion` and the `marketingConsentVersion`.
- **House style:** found no em or en dashes, no straight apostrophes in visible copy, no US spellings and no lowercase “autistic”. Spaced hyphens are used as dashes in 10 places (rows 18, 19, 34, 42, 46, 50, 53, 57, 60, 64).
