# Change register 04: Sitewide chrome, metadata, alt text, resources, CTAs and confirmations

Scope: navigation and footer on every public page (`git ls-files '*.html' | grep -v '^docs/'`, review files excluded), `<title>`, meta and og tags on every page, image alt text, resource and tool introductions (frameworks/, the Sovereignty Conditions Audit, free2bme/#reflection, the-practice/ intro), every button and CTA destination, the four confirmation pages, a sitewide keyword sweep, and the film drafts in `review/films/all-of-this-is-me/`.

Method: scripted extraction of nav, footer, CTA, meta and `<img>` markup from every page; an internal link and anchor checker (query strings and `_redirects` taken into account); the photographs checked by eye; a keyword sweep of visible text. No site files were edited.

Overlap: registers 01 and 02 already cover page body copy on home, about, media, recognition, The Nest, speaking, professional pages and frameworks. Where they already have an item, this register points to it ("see 01-#n" / "see 02-#n") and doesn’t repeat it. `about/`, `index.html`, `media/`, `recognition/` and `the-nest/` were being edited in the working tree during this audit, so wording quoted from those files is as it stood at the time of checking.

Link check result: **no broken internal links or anchors.** Every `href`/`src` resolves to a tracked file, and every `#fragment` (`#enquiry`, `#reserve`, `#learning`, `#reflection`, `#signature-talk`, `#f-world` and others) exists. `?type=` and `?book=` preselects match the scripts in `work-with-tanya/` and `assets/js/book-preorder.js`.

## Film captions: governing-message check (no wording changes)

`draft1.en.vtt` and the NOTES transcript are Tanya’s spoken words and must stay as spoken. Nothing in them needs changing. Notes for her review:

- "Acceptance for all of you… accepting all of it" fits the governing message well, and "…embracing who I am" is a strong opening (NOTES question 1).
- "one of the beautiful things about mindfulness is acceptance" puts acceptance in a mindfulness and yoga frame. That's accurate to the talk. But if the homepage film is the first thing visitors meet, it could read as a mindset or wellbeing message. The on-page copy around the film (the "All of this is me" lines) carries the wider meaning, so keep those lines next to the film.
- "that’s what I’ve been on a journey to do, and I’ve had an amazing team of different people that have helped me" can't change. It usefully shows that acceptance takes support, not solo effort. Don't caption or frame it as an achievement arc.
- Caption cues 7 and 8 split "try and / boot it out the door" across a line break. Consider re-timing so "try and boot it out the door," sits on one cue. Timing only, no word change.
- The NOTES ask Tanya to check "accepting, getting the heart bigger" by ear. That still needs doing before the captions are final.

## Keyword sweep (all public pages, visible text)

Terms searched: overcom*, despite, inspir*, brave*, courag*, fix*, recover*, confiden*, mindset, deficit*, resilien*, warrior, battl*, struggl*, suffer*, empower*, award*, achiev*, success*, productiv*, journey, thriv*, potential.

- **No hits** for overcome, despite, inspir*, brave, courage, mindset, warrior, battle or suffer in live page text. The old media og:description ("She has not overcome these realities…") has already been replaced in the working tree.

- **deficit:** every hit rejects deficit framing (transcript, systems-change H1 on an unserved page, media "zero deficit talk"). No problem.
- **Awards / achievement:** recognition/ and the homepage list award names as facts. Award titles such as "Overcoming the Odds", "Most Resilient Women-Owned Business" and "Achievement in Diversity &amp; Inclusion" are proper names and should stay. - **journey:** transcript ("not a journey away from help") and the film. No problem.
- **potential:** practitioner-pathway "A supported bridge between potential and responsibility." It's about practitioners, not worth. No problem.
- **Narrowing to disability:** the speaking meta lists disability first, among leadership, belonging, support and work (02-#15 covers it). The media tags start with "Disability" in a list of nine.
