## Questions for Tanya

1. **About story, ordinary life.** What does an ordinary good day look like for you? A food, a place, a song, a habit or a person (no names needed) would let the About page show pleasure that has nothing to do with work or achievement. Nothing like this is in the repo yet.
2. **Hockey.** The media bio says you "previously played hockey for Australia". Can the About page say more (which team or level, roughly when, what you loved about it)? Rows 15, 28 and 42 use only the existing sentence.
3. **Tammy and Yoga Nrg.** The film notes record your mindfulness and yoga training with Tammy from Yoga Nrg. May the About page name Tammy or Yoga Nrg, or should it say "mindfulness and yoga training" only (row 29 uses the unnamed version)? Is the training a formal qualification, or learning with a mentor?
4. **Family in third-party bios.** "Single mother of a transgender daughter" is public on Home and About. Do you want it in the media bio and kit, which journalists and organisers republish (row 42)? Does your daughter want it there?
5. **Kianni Jackson.** Girl of the Year (third place, Paris 2026) is listed under your recognition and awarded to Kianni Jackson. Should visitors be told who Kianni is and how she connects to your work, or should that stay private?
6. **Contribution at The Nest.** Do people at The Nest make things, run sessions, share skills or help shape the space? Row 63 is held until you confirm what is true.
7. **Partner responsibilities.** For Headspace Caloundra’s Rainbow Social Group, is it accurate that Headspace is responsible for running the group, deciding who it is for and handling sign-ups, while Neurodivergent Empowered provides the venue? Row 12 states this, and it needs your confirmation before it goes live.
8. **Partner name styling.** Headspace styles its own name in lower case ("headspace"). Do you want the site to follow that?
9. **Women’s empowerment on the homepage.** Section `#f-women` now speaks to "people of all genders", and the word "women" no longer appears there, though the media bio lists women’s empowerment as a core interest and About has a strong statement on women’s inclusion. Should the homepage name women’s empowerment (inclusive of trans women) again, or is the all-genders framing deliberate?
