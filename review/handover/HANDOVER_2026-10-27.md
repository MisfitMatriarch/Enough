## 5. Contracts and connected pieces (do not break)

- **Netlify forms.** These form names are detected by Netlify and route enquiries: `speaking-booking` (speaking/ and work-with-tanya/), `book-preorders`, `be-first-to-know`, `free2bme-updates`, `supervision-interest`, `associate-interest`. Do not rename them, or rename or remove their fields or hidden `form-name` inputs.
- **Book reservations.** `netlify/functions/expo-reservations.mjs` uses `@netlify/blobs` and stores **real people's names and emails**. Do not modify it, read stored data or change storage without Tanya's explicit approval.
- **URL parameters.** `?type=` (`keynote`, `mc`, `panel`, `workshop` on speaking/; others on work-with-tanya/) and `?book=<slug>` on books/pre-order/ preselect form options. Links depend on them.
- **Build.** `scripts/build-expo.mjs` copies the site to `dist/` and excludes `internalDirectories` and internal file types. Netlify publishes `dist/`. New internal notes go in `review/` or `docs/`.
- **Acknowledgement of Country gate.** sessionStorage key `f2bm-aoc=1`. Do not edit the Acknowledgement wording; it is printed identically in all eight books.
- **`_redirects`.** Preserves old book and page URLs. Do not remove entries.
- **Menu and footer.** The same markup is repeated in all 39 public HTML pages. A change must be applied to every page consistently, then verified on several pages.
- **Videos** in `assets/video/` are faststart MP4s with `.vtt` captions. Captions must match Tanya's spoken words, verified by listening.
